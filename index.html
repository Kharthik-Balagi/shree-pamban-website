05 / Helpful answers

Frequently Asked
Questions.

Everything you need to know before your little one begins their Shree Pamban journey.

What age groups do you welcome?

We welcome children from 3 years onwards for Pre KG, LKG, and UKG. We also offer Playgroup and Day care options.

What are your school timings?

Our team will share the current batch timings and available Day care schedules when you book a visit.

Can I visit the school before enrolling?

Yes. Book a free school visit through the form and we will confirm a convenient date and time with you.

What does learning look like at Shree Pamban?

Children learn through play, stories, music, movement, hands-on activities, celebrations, and caring everyday routines.

Do you provide Day care?

Yes. Our Day care provides a safe, nurturing second home for little ones. Contact us for availability and details.
